Choose sockets and orientations for three logical cameras. The final placement must see all glyph boards and provide redundant coverage for the highlighted high-risk boards.

## Why It Matters

It turns camera planning into a constrained coverage problem that mixes spatial reasoning with combinatorial optimization.
