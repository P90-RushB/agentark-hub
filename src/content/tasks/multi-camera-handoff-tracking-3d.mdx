## Task Brief

Follow token T1 through the CAM A, CAM B, and CAM C video feeds, preserve its identity through opaque handoff tunnels, assign its exit, open the matching gate, and submit.

## Why It Matters

The task probes object permanence and cross-camera tracking when continuous visual contact is deliberately broken.
