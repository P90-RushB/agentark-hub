## Task Brief

Actively steer the available PTZ feeds, inspect a limited number of views, combine the revealed structural clues, and mark the target that satisfies the query.

## Why It Matters

The task evaluates closed-loop visual search: an agent must select viewpoints, interpret evidence, and know when it has enough information to decide.
