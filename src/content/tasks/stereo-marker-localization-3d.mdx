## Task Brief

Combine the two calibrated camera feeds, disambiguate the target glyph, and infer its grid coordinates. Place the probe at the estimated location and submit the localization.

## Why It Matters

It tests whether an agent can turn perspective differences into a precise spatial decision rather than relying on a single ambiguous view.
