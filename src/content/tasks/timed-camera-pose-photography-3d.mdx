## Task Brief

Position a floating camera to capture each exhibit during a brief appearance. Keep the entire exhibit large and clearly visible across its photo burst.

## Start a session

This mod includes a task launcher. After extracting the platform ZIP, read `launch/LAUNCH.md` for the toolchain and startup command. Use `launch/launch_task.py` to prepare the requested instance and open AgentArk. The launcher preserves this task's original seeds, observations, and scoring.

The Linux verification used `--unity-start-timeout-s 2400` for this task's initialization and communication. The default is 240 seconds; this option does not change the in-game clock, action budget, or scoring.
