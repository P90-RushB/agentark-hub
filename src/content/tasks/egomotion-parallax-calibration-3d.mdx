## Task Brief

Run one paid motion probe, compare the sampled video frames, classify the camera's translation, yaw, or orbit direction and magnitude, then apply the inverse compensation.

## Why It Matters

It tests temporal camera reasoning through parallax and motion cues rather than static object recognition.
