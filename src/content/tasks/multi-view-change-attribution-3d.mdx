## Task Brief

Capture the baseline, compare it with the changed state across three labeled feeds, identify the altered camera anchor and the independently moved object, then restore both transformations.

## Why It Matters

It tests causal visual attribution: the agent must distinguish changes caused by the observer from changes in the observed world.
